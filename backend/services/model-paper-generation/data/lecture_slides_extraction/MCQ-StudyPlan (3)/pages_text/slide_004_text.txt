3. Student Quiz Evaluation and Progress Analysis
This page displays the student progress report after completing the recommended 
MCQ quiz. The system evaluates student responses and calculates performance metrics 
such as total questions attempted, number of correct answers, incorrect answers, and 
overall accuracy. Additionally, lecture-wise performance analysis helps identify topics where 
the student demonstrates strengths or weaknesses.
