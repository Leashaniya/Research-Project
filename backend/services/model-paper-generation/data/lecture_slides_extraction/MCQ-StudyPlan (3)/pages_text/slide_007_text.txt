This is to confirm that the developed component functions in relation to the DMS module. 
The system analyzes DMS lecture slides and past paper MID MCQ questions using Natural 
Language Processing techniques. It performs semantic mapping between lecture topics and 
exam questions, conducts frequency-based analysis of past papers, and generates baseline 
and adaptive study plans to support exam preparation. The system also evaluates student 
quiz performance and applies a graph-based representation to model relationships between 
lectures, topics, and MCQ questions for visualization and recommendation.
