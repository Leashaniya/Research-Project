2ND NORMAL FORM
A relation R is in second normal form (2NF) if every nonprime attribute A in R is not partially 
dependent on any key of R.
Second normal form (2NF) is based on the concept of full functional dependency.

A functional dependency X →Y is a full functional dependency if removal of any attribute A from X means that 
the dependency does not hold any more.

For example, in a relation R (ABCDE) where AB -> {ABCDE}, if A-> C , A->C is a partial dependency (not fully 
functional dependent)
