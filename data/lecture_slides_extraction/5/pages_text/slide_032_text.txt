T-SQL: TRIGGER SYNTAX (CONTD.)
FOR|AFTER
AFTER specifies that the DML trigger is fired only when all operations specified in the triggering 
SQL statement have executed successfully.
AFTER is the default when FOR is the only keyword specified.
AFTER triggers cannot be defined on views.
INSTEAD OF
Specifies that the trigger is executed instead of the triggering SQL statement, thus overriding the 
actions of the triggering statements.
Specifies At most, one INSTEAD OF trigger per INSERT, UPDATE, or DELETE statement can be 
defined on a table or view.
